**Nahida LP Tracker** is a Discord bot designed to organize, manage, and illustrate a community-driven "LP Race" (Ranked climbing competition) in League of Legends. It connects players by dividing them into two competing teams (**Team Caméléon** vs. **Team Dragon**) and tracks their Ranked progression over a set period. 

The primary goal is to encourage friends and community members to play together, improve their gameplay, and engage in friendly competition without the usual "ranked anxiety."

---

## ✨ Core Features & Community Value

* **Zero-Anxiety Scoring System:** To promote a positive player experience and reduce ranked anxiety, individual LP losses are displayed on a player's personal profile but count as `+0 LP` toward the global team score. This encourages players to keep queuing and trying to improve without the fear of dragging their team down.
* **Strict Anti-Smurf Enforcement:** To protect the competitive integrity of the event, the bot automatically rejects accounts that show signs of smurfing. Requirements include a minimum of 20 ranked games with a winrate below 80%, and a minimum account level of 100 (with specific algorithmic exceptions for highly active players).
* **Automated Team Balancing:** The bot ensures fair play by automatically reassigning registering players if the population difference between the two teams exceeds two members.
* **Dynamic Visual Profiles (Canvas):** Utilizing `@napi-rs/canvas`, the bot generates custom, visually distinct player cards and team leaderboards. These graphics celebrate player progression and display live stats (Wins, Losses, Winrate, LP Gain) using embedded Windows fonts for cross-platform consistency.
* **Automated Snapshot Leaderboards:** A silent cron job captures daily data at 19:00, and a comprehensive graphical leaderboard is published automatically at 20:00 (Daily, Weekly, or Final standings).

## 🛠️ Bot Commands

### 👤 Player Commands
* `/rejoindre <riot_id> <tag> <team> <region>`: Registers the player for the event and links their Riot account (subject to Anti-Smurf validation).
* `/profil [@member]`: Generates a personalized Canvas image showcasing the player's live Ranked evolution since the start of the event.
* `/timeleft`: Displays the remaining duration of the active LP Race.

### 🛡️ Administration Commands
* `/start-race` & `/eventstop`: Initializes a new event or officially closes an active one (announcing the MVP and winning team).
* `/force-ajout <member> <riot_id> <tag> <team> <region>`: Manual database registration to bypass anti-smurf restrictions for trusted edge cases.
* `/supprimer-joueur <member>`: Completely purges a player and their snapshot history from the database.
* `/changer-equipe <member> <team>`: Manually transfers a player to the opposing team.
* `/dailylb`, `/weeklylb`, `/finallb`: Manual triggers to generate and broadcast live visual leaderboards ahead of the automated schedule.

## ⚙️ Technical API Integration & Rate Limiting

This project is built using **Node.js, discord.js (v14), and Better-SQLite3**. It queries the following Riot Games API endpoints:
* `SUMMONER-V4`: Used to verify account levels (Anti-Smurf) and fetch the current Profile Icon ID.
* `LEAGUE-V4`: Used to fetch live Ranked Solo/Duo stats (Wins, Losses, current LP, and Tier) to calculate player evolution.

**Rate Limit Strategy:** The bot handles user requests asynchronously. For automated daily leaderboards, database queries are processed sequentially to prevent bulk endpoint spamming. The architecture ensures that global traffic strictly adheres to the standard developer limits (20 requests every 1 second, 100 requests every 2 minutes).
